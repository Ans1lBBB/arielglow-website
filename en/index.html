The Rhythm
of Speed & Art

I love school and I love walking fast. I practice racewalking on the track during the day, then curl up at my perler bead desk after school. Both make me so happy I often forget to stop.

3000m Racewalk · Meet Record Holder

19:51.70

At the 2025 Changhua County Citizens Sports Meet, I represented Huatan Township in the elementary girls' 3000m racewalk. I was nervous for days before the race. My legs hurt the whole way, and I only found out at the finish that I'd gotten first place and broken the meet record... I'm still a little shy saying that out loud, and I can hardly believe it when I think back. But my heart felt light, and I just kept smiling.

Racewalking looks slow, but the rhythm is actually fast. Perler beads look tiny, but bead by bead they turn into a really big world.

I try hard with school, training, and beading too. When I like something, I'm happy to spend a long time on it and do it bit by bit.

Pixel by Pixel

These are little pieces I beaded one pixel at a time. Each one has its size listed. If you like one, you can browse my little perler bead shop and pick one to take home.

I want to tell you something first…

My shop just opened. I made one ready piece of each design, kind of like saving it for whoever loves it first.

If it's still in stock, I'll wrap it carefully and take it to 7-11 to ship. It usually gets to your store in about three or four days.

If someone already took that one, I'll bead a new one for you, one pixel at a time. Between homework and practice it takes about 5 to 10 days. When it's done I'll ship it as soon as I can. Please wait for me a little!

Whether it's in stock is written clearly in the shop. If you're not sure, email me. I'll read it carefully and write back.

Want to talk to me?

Ask if a piece is still available, when a custom one might be ready, or just send me a cheer. I'd love that. I'll read your message and write back for real.
