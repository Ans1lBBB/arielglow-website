Books by day, fast feet on the track after school, then perler beads until bedtime. Ariel Bi loves all three, and often forgets to stop.

Changhua County Citizens Sports Meet · 3000m Racewalk Record

19:51.70

I'm Ariel Bi. At the 2025 Changhua County Citizens Sports Meet, athletes from every township and city in the county came together to compete. I represented Huatan Township in the elementary girls' 3000m racewalk, won gold, and set a new meet record for Changhua County. My legs were on fire at the finish, but I kept smiling anyway.

Racewalking looks slow from the outside, but the rhythm is fast. Perler beads look tiny, but bead by bead they grow into a whole world.

I'm a Pisces, so I daydream a lot while I work. Schoolwork gets my full attention, and so do training and beading. When Ariel Bi loves something, she shows up for it one step at a time.

A quick note before you browse…

The shop just opened. Ariel Bi keeps one ready-made piece of each design, like a little gift for whoever loves it first.

If it's still in stock, she packs it carefully and drops it off at 7-11 for shipping. It usually reaches your chosen store in about three or four days.

If someone already took that one, she'll bead a fresh piece for you, one pixel at a time. Between homework and practice it takes about 5 to 10 days, then she ships it as soon as it's done.
